Wizard101 contains two key ingredients for zone interaction: volumes and triggers. Volumes are a physical
representation in the zone. When a player enters or exists that volume, it can "trigger" an event. this is
what the Trigger type is for.

Volumes:
============
This class derives from CoreObjectInfo. Surprisingly, they do contain a template ID. However, the m_templateID
field from it's parent class is unused. There is a duplicate member for the template ID.
Notable fields:
- m_enterEvents
- m_exitEvents
- **m_location                    | THIS FIELD IS UNUSED,
- m_locationX
- m_locationY
- m_locationZ
- m_radius
- **m_templateID (CoreObjectInfo) | THIS FIELD IS UNUSED.
- m_templateID (Volume)
- m_volumeName

Triggers:
============
These are just results with extra data attached. Said extra data is useless because I have no idea what it
stands for. WizUnraveler guessed this type, so there's a good chance the data might not even be getting
deserialized correctly. In good news though, the only data we really need out of them we already have.
Notable fields:
- m_results
- m_triggerName
- m_volumes | This is just a list of what volumes trigger this event.

It is possible to tell what volumes are zone transfers. The trigger for the volume will contain a single result, `ResTeleport`. 
Unfortunately, the data is not serialized with client.
